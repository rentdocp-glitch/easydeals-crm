Privacy Policy

Proptech EasyDeals (Easy Deals), Pune, Maharashtra, India. Last updated: 22 November 2026.

Easy Deals helps people buy and sell resale properties in Pune. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and your choices.

What we collect

When you enquire about a property (on a property portal such as 99acres, Magicbricks or Housing.com, on our website, by phone or on WhatsApp), we receive your name, phone number, the property you asked about, and your requirement. If you list your property with us, we also keep the property and owner details you give us. Messages you send us on WhatsApp are stored so that our team can reply.

How we use it

We use this information only to contact you about the property you enquired about, answer your questions, arrange site visits, and manage the sale or purchase. We may use WhatsApp, phone calls and SMS for this. We do not use your information for unrelated advertising.

Who we share it with

Our own team members who handle your enquiry. When you ask for a site visit, we may share your name and visit time with the property owner. We use trusted service providers to run our systems (cloud database and messaging, including Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform). We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time and we will stop messaging you. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us using the details below.

Security and retention

Your information is stored in access-controlled systems and only authorised team members can see it. We keep enquiry records only as long as needed for the transaction and for legal and accounting purposes, and then delete or anonymise them.
