## WhatsApp cost facts (checked 25 Nov 2026)

- Meta charges per **template** message delivered. Replies that are not templates are free inside an open 24-hour customer-service window, and utility templates sent inside that window are also free. Source: [Meta pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing).
- Meta raised the India marketing rate on 1 Jan 2026. I could not read the exact INR rate card from here, so no ₹ figure is given. Check it in Meta Business Manager before any API sending.
- **Action for you (free):** eligible Indian businesses must move their WhatsApp account billing to INR by **31 Dec 2026**, or Meta stops delivering their messages from 1 Jan 2027.
